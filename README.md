# Manuscript and data: Universal spectrum and scaling laws for dark matter halo mass functions and density profiles

Z. Xu (2026), *Universal spectrum and scaling laws for dark matter halo mass functions and density profiles*, Research Square preprint, [doi:10.21203/rs.3.rs-8012958/v3](https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-8012958/v3); arXiv: [2109.09985](https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.09985).

This repository holds the manuscript, the plotted data behind every figure, and the recomputed results of the paper.

## Major findings (dark matter halo mass functions and density profiles)

- A characteristic halo mass m<sub>h</sub>* ∝ t marks a linear-to-nonlinear transition; near it gravity organizes the matter field into a quasi-universal power spectrum P(k) ∝ k<sup>−1</sup>, independent of the primordial spectrum.
- Five critical scales reorder at a crossover a<sub>*</sub>, when the cutoff mass M* of the mass function overtakes m<sub>h</sub>*; the universal range forms only after this crossover.
- The two halo masses follow two diffusion clocks: m<sub>h</sub>* follows a cascade clock that slows at a<sub>*</sub>, and M* follows a linear clock that does not.
- In mass space, haloes random-walk with waiting time τ<sub>g</sub> ∝ m<sub>h</sub><sup>−λ</sup>, giving a double-λ mass function with an exponential cutoff at λ<sub>2</sub> = 2/3 and a small-mass slope λ<sub>1</sub> = 5/6.
- Within haloes, a radial cascade at rate ε = ε<sub>u</sub>(m<sub>h</sub>/m<sub>h</sub>*)<sup>2/3</sup> gives ρ ∝ r<sup>−4/3</sup> inside the scale radius r<sub>s</sub>; the front of a particle random walk sets the cutoff radius R<sub>h</sub>.
- Six halo radii, read through three density laws, have scalings that agree with Illustris-1-Dark.
- Kinetic energy cascades inversely at ε<sub>u</sub> and potential energy directly at −1.4 ε<sub>u</sub>; the total energy is then dissipated by cosmic expansion at −0.4 ε<sub>u</sub>. The energy cascade rate is |ε<sub>u</sub>| ≈ (2–5)×10<sup>−7</sup> m² s<sup>−3</sup>.
- Small-scale permanence: the halo group mass and the −4/3 inner profile of haloes at m<sub>h</sub>*(z) do not change with time, and the mass cascade rate ε<sub>m</sub> ∝ a<sup>−1</sup>.
